## Iteration 1. The e-value, the strata, the validity gates, tests

**OBJECTIVE.** Implement the pre-registered e-process exactly: the e-value of a stratum, the deterministic assignment of control windows (section 4, rules 1 to 3), the completion rule (rule 4), and gates 1 and 5 of section 8.

**PROBLEM IDENTIFIED.** The strata must be a pure function of the calendar (release list, release days, stream start, data end), so that tests can check them without market data and so that the status part can decide, before any download, whether anything may be computed. Eastern day arithmetic must hold across the daylight saving changes (a control four weeks after an October release is in November, an hour later in UTC).

**HYPOTHESIS.** Lemma 1 holds exactly (gate 1); under exchangeable strata of the planned sizes, the share of sequences on which M ever reaches 20 is below 5% (gate 5), and well below it, because Ville's bound is not attained by a discrete process that overshoots the threshold.

**CHANGES IMPLEMENTED.**
* `experiments/e25_sequential_events.py`: `e_value`, `e_value_oracle`, `e_process`; `et_anchor` (Eastern clock time on an Eastern day, to UTC, through the IANA database); `assign_strata` (rules 1 to 3, one row per candidate with the reason it was dropped), `strata_table`, `completion` (rule 4 and the complete prefix: strata enter in the order i, so a complete stratum after an incomplete one waits), `window_overlaps`; `window_outcomes` (an up alarm in [anchor, anchor + 6 h)); parts `theory` and `strata`.
* `tests/test_e25.py` (29 tests, synthetic, about 2 s).

**EXPERIMENTS PERFORMED.**
* `python experiments/e25_sequential_events.py theory` (5 s): gate 1 over n = 0 to 8, S = 0 to n + 1 and ψ in {2, 4, 8, 16} (216 cases), with rational arithmetic over every arrangement of the S windows with the outcome, and in floating point with the code's `e_value`; gate 5 with the strata sizes of the planning calendar to 1 January 2029 (41 strata, cycled to 200), 20,000 sequences per law, seeds `[25000, i]`.
* `python experiments/e25_sequential_events.py strata` (under 1 s).

**RESULTS.**
* [PROVED, checked exactly] Gate 1: the conditional mean of e(ψ) given S under H0 is exactly 1 in all 216 cases in rational arithmetic; the largest floating-point deviation is 8.9 × 10⁻¹⁶ (`results/e25_theory_identity.csv`).
* [SYNTHETIC] Gate 5 (`results/e25_validity.csv`): P(M ever ≥ 20 within 200 releases) is 0.028, 0.030, 0.032 and 0.036 for exchangeable Bernoulli strata at rates 5%, 10%, 20% and 40%; 0 for the all-or-nothing law (every e-value is 1); 0.032 when exactly one window per stratum has the outcome; 0.034 when S is uniform on 0 to n + 1; 0.046 when S is chosen from the earlier strata (S_i = 1 after a release without the outcome, S_i = n_i after one with it). All are below the limit 0.0546 (5% plus three Monte Carlo standard errors at 5%). At 1/α = 100 the shares are 0.005 to 0.009 (bound 0.01) and at 1/α = 10 they are 0.058 to 0.094 (bound 0.10), apart from the all-or-nothing law (0). The mean of M after one and after ten strata is 1 within two standard errors in every law; after 200 strata the sample mean is far below 1 in most laws (0.15 to 0.34), because a test martingale's mean is carried by rare, very large values; this is expected and not a failure.
* Planned forward calendar (`results/e25_strata_planned.csv`; the event file's released and scheduled rows minus 27 January 2027, no data end): 23 strata to December 2027 with 3 to 8 controls each (125 kept of 184 candidates). The first stratum, the FOMC statement of 28 October 2026, keeps 3 controls (4, 18 and 25 November 2026, 14:00 EST; its four earlier candidates precede the stream start plus 1800 minutes, and 11 November is one day from the CPI release of 10 November) and completes on 26 November 2026 at 01:00 UTC. Rule 3 drops 16 candidates (two each from eight strata).
* **A finding about the design (not changed):** 30 pairs of control windows of different strata overlap by 30 minutes: a CPI control at 08:30 ET and an FOMC control at 14:00 ET on the same Wednesday share the half hour from 14:00 to 14:30 ET. Rule 3 makes every window belong to one stratum, as the pre-registration says ("No window is used twice"), but the windows are not disjoint in time, so an up alarm in that half hour counts for two strata. Lemma 1 and Theorem 1 hold under H0 as stated; what changes is how plausible H0 is, in the sense of Remark 1 of the theory note: given an earlier stratum's FOMC control with the outcome, the later stratum's CPI control on the same day is a little more likely to have it than the release window. Reported to the coordinator with a proposed fix; the committed design is implemented as written.

**IMPROVEMENTS.** The assignment, the completion rule and the refusal are checked on synthetic calendars, including the daylight saving changes of March and November, the 2-day rule at exactly 1 and 2 days, the 1800-minute start rule at 1799 and 1800 minutes, the data-end rule at 359 and 360 minutes, a candidate used by an earlier release, and a release list given out of order.

**REMAINING ISSUES and failures recorded.**
* The first draft reported the mean of M after 200 strata as a validity check; replaced by the means after 1 and 10 strata (the 200-strata mean is kept in the CSV with the explanation above).
* A test of the status refusal first assumed that a stratum is complete when its data are complete; the code was right (a scheduled event-file row within 30 days also blocks completion) and the test was corrected.
* An empty release list made pandas read an empty Boolean list as a column selection; fixed with an explicit Boolean array.

## Iteration 2. Gates 2 and 3 on known data; power (section 9)

**OBJECTIVE.** Show on known data that the S2 alarms before any time do not depend on later data (gate 2) and that E25's code computes E23's committed release outcomes (gate 3); compute the power of the e-process under F24a's planning model (section 9).

**PROBLEM IDENTIFIED.** The status part will run S2 over all data to a look; its alarms before 1 June 2027 must equal those of F24a's run, and its outcomes must be those of E12's definition. The power section asks for crossing probabilities at three dates, the releases to the crossing, and the cost of estimating the control rate from at most 8 controls.

**HYPOTHESIS.** S2 and E12's deseasonalisation are causal (the intraday profile uses past days only), so a run whose data end at a cut-off gives the same alarms before it. E25 is less powerful than F24a on 1 June 2027 and catches up later.

**CHANGES IMPLEMENTED.** `run_arm` (one run of an arm on one coin and period, exactly as `E22.job` runs it: `E12.load_coin` through E23's period switch, `E22.make_arm`, `E22.run_monitor`, `E12.alarm_frame`); parts `gates` and `power`; `planning_table` (F24a's planning calendar from `E24.planning_events`, with E25's strata and the time each stratum enters the test, assuming every event-file row is updated at its release time); `simulate_power` (common random numbers across scenarios); `expected_log_growth` (exact); figure `e25_power.png`.

**RESULTS.**

## Iteration 3. The post hoc illustration (section 10; seen data, exploratory)

**OBJECTIVE.** Show what the e-process does on releases the project has already seen: S2 on BTC, ETH and SOL, E22's period (21 January 2024 to 31 December 2025) and E23's period (1 January to 24 October 2026), strata built by section 4 within each period.

**PROBLEM IDENTIFIED.** These data were seen (E12, E14, E22, E23) before E25 was designed, and the 2024 to 2025 release contrast is the one that motivated the forward tests; nothing here can be evidence. The illustration answers only how the procedure behaves: how often it would have crossed, how early, and what the 2026 releases, on which E23's primary test failed, look like through it.

**HYPOTHESIS.** On 2024 to 2025, where S2's release rate was 61% against 15% for matched controls (E22), M crosses 20 early; on 2026, where E23 found 6 of 16 BTC releases against 10.9% (p = 0.007 for S2, a secondary family), the evidence is weaker.

**CHANGES IMPLEMENTED.** Part `posthoc` (`period_strata`, outcomes from the committed alarm files `results/e22_alarms.csv` and `results/e23_alarms.csv`, used only because gate 3 holds; otherwise it reruns S2), figure `e25_posthoc.png`. Rule 2 uses every released or rescheduled row of each period's event file (so the FOMC statement of 28 October 2026, after E23's data end, still excludes nearby candidates); rule 4 is applied with the period's end as the data end, so the last strata of each period are not complete and do not enter (E22: the FOMC statement of 10 December 2025 and the CPI release of 18 December 2025; E23: the CPI release of 14 October 2026).

**EXPERIMENTS PERFORMED.** `nice -n 10 python experiments/e25_sequential_events.py posthoc` (2 s; no market data read, gate 3 read from `results/e25_gates.csv`).

**RESULTS.** [REAL, post hoc, exploratory] (`results/e25_posthoc_summary.csv`, `results/e25_posthoc_strata.csv`.)
* 2024 to 2025, 36 complete strata per coin (2 to 8 controls, median 4.5; 161 control windows): BTC 21 of 36 releases with the outcome against 26 of 161 controls (16.1%); M first reaches 20 at the 9th stratum (the FOMC statement of 12 June 2024) and ends at 4.3 × 10⁵. ETH 20 of 36 against 16.8%, first crossing 31 July 2024 (stratum 11), final 8.5 × 10³ (40 crossed on 14 August 2024). SOL 20 of 36 against 14.3%, first crossing 12 June 2024 (stratum 8), final 8.4 × 10⁴.
* 2026, 15 complete strata per coin (4 to 8 controls, median 6; 85 control windows): BTC 6 of 15 against 10 of 85 (11.8%), final M = 6.7, never 20; ETH 7 of 15 against 12.9%, final 13.1; SOL 7 of 15 against 12.9%, final 15.9. BTC FOMC statements alone (exploratory): 4 of 6, final 19.6. BTC's M fell to 0.22 after four CPI and FOMC releases without the outcome in January to March 2026 and rose with the releases of July to September.
* On 2026 BTC, the fixed-sample stratified test (E23's S2 row, a secondary family, p = 0.007) and the e-process (6.7, below 20) differ in the way the power part predicts: Ville's threshold costs power at a fixed sample, and E25's strata keep fewer controls (median 6 against 29 to 39 per release in E23's design, `results/e23_windows.csv`).

**IMPROVEMENTS.** None to the design (it is fixed).

**REMAINING ISSUES.** Two releases on the same day (12 June 2024, CPI at 08:30 and FOMC at 14:00 ET) have release windows that overlap by 30 minutes, the same mechanism as the overlapping control windows of iteration 1; this cannot happen in the forward calendar to December 2027 (no CPI release and FOMC statement share a day), but it can after.
