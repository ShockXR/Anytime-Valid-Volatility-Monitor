## Iteration 1. Design and pre-registration

**OBJECTIVE.** Answer finding J1 of the referee's second report (`docs/review/referee_report_paper_round2.md`): rerun E12's pre-specified primary test (FOMC and CPI on BTC, streaming design) with a monitor configuration that carries a proved per-day false alarm bound, and say what follows.

**PROBLEM IDENTIFIED.** Every real-data analysis, including E12's primary test (classified as the only pre-specified primary test in E20), used the plug-in baseline, which has no finite-sample guarantee (E14, Proposition A.6). The abstract nevertheless used the FOMC result to show "what the guarantee buys".

**HYPOTHESIS.** If the FOMC contrast comes from the releases and not from the plug-in's estimation error, it should survive a monitor whose per-day false alarm probability is bounded: the confidence-sequence baseline restarted at every UTC day boundary (Corollary 1 of `docs/theory_per_period.md`). A possible failure mode, known from E19, is that confidence-sequence segments can be blind to increases when τ² ≤ m_up.

**CHANGES IMPLEMENTED.** `docs/preregistration_e22.md`, committed alone as `949c1d5` (05:59 UTC, 3 November 2026) before anything was run on real data. It fixes: the arms P1 (primary; `PeriodicVolatilityMonitor(baseline="cs", delta=0.01, A = 36,000, period=1440)`, restart at 00:00 UTC), S1 (the same without restart), S2 (E14's awake rank monitor) and P0 (E12's plug-in, reproduction); the guarantee of each arm and its hypothesis; the families F22a (primary: P1, BTC, FOMC and CPI) to F22e with Holm within each; the decision rule (FOMC on BTC under P1, Holm p ≤ 0.05); the diagnostics (per-day alarm rates against the bounds, releases watched with E19's blind-segment criterion); the reproduction gate; and the reading if the primary test passes or fails. It discloses what had been seen: E12's results, E14 Q14g's published S2 result, and E19's committed confidence-sequence alarm lists that overlap S1.

**EXPERIMENTS PERFORMED.** Before the commit, a synthetic check only (scratch file outside the repository): P1, S1 and S2 on a 20-day i.i.d. Gaussian stream with one burst, to confirm that `PeriodicVolatilityMonitor(baseline="cs")` runs E12's design as it stands (it calibrates like `VolatilityMonitor(baseline="cs")` and restarts both statistics at every period boundary after calibration) and to time the monitors (8, 8 and 31 µs per step). Reading the code also showed that each UTC day's monitored minutes belong to one segment, since recalibration takes 1440 minutes, so each day is one period of Corollary 1. One long-run statement is derived in the pre-registration rather than quoted: Corollary 6.6 of `docs/math.md` applies to the restarted monitor, because a restart only lowers the current statistic and hence the supermartingale of its proof.

**RESULTS.** Design fixed. No substitute configuration was needed.

**IMPROVEMENTS.** The configuration, test, families and reading are on record before any result.

**REMAINING ISSUES.** The data are not new: E12's FOMC result was known when E22 was designed.

**NEXT ITERATION.** Script, tests on synthetic data, reproduction gate, run.

## Iteration 2. Script, synthetic tests, reproduction, run

**OBJECTIVE.** Run the four arms on E12's streams with only the monitor changed, after checking that the plug-in arm reproduces E12 exactly.

**PROBLEM IDENTIFIED.** The comparison is meaningful only if everything except the monitor is E12's own code and the plug-in arm, rerun through the new script, gives E12's published numbers.

**HYPOTHESIS.** Importing E12's functions (`read_events`, `load_coin`, `control_anchors`, `window_stats`, `alarm_frame`, `stratified_tests`, `holm`, `wilson`) and E12's monitoring loop reproduces E12 to the last digit; S2 through E14's `make_monitor` with E14's seed reproduces E14 Q14g.

**CHANGES IMPLEMENTED.**
* `experiments/e22_guaranteed_events.py`: `make_arm` (the four registered constructors), `utc_day_offset` (offset of the daily restart; stops if the minute index has a gap), `run_monitor` (E12's loop), `segment_table` and `watched_flags` (segments, E19's criterion τ² ≤ m_up for an increase side that cannot alarm), `period_counts` and `first_day` (per-day alarm shares against the bounds), `long_run_bound_per_monitored_day`, `reproduction` and `s2_against_e14` (cell-by-cell comparison with E12's and E14's CSVs read at round-trip precision), `add_families` (Holm within the registered families), `watched_table`, `figure`. Part `reproduce` runs P0 only; part `run` runs P0 first and stops unless it matches E12 exactly, then P1, S1 and S2 (two worker processes, `nice -n 10`).
* `tests/test_e22.py` (9 tests, synthetic only): offset and gap check; the registered constructors and their 5% budgets; P1 restarts at every UTC midnight after calibration and equals S1 before the first boundary; a segment whose quiet first quarter makes τ² ≤ m_up is monitored but not watched and raises no up alarm (P1 and S1); the rank monitor is never blind and starts a new segment right after each alarm; period and first-day counts; the long-run bounds; the Holm families (sizes 2, 4, 6, 6, 2; one primary test); the comparison function flags a difference of 1e-15.
* The figure was laid out on synthetic placeholder numbers first (scratch file, not kept), then drawn from the real results. Colours: categorical slots 1 and 2 of the reference palette, checked with the palette validator; filled against open markers as a second cue.

**EXPERIMENTS PERFORMED.**
1. `nice -n 10 python experiments/e22_guaranteed_events.py reproduce --procs=2` (06:04 UTC, 15 s): P0 against `e12_tests.csv` (60 rows: all outcomes, both samples, 3 coins, 2 release types; 14 columns), E12's primary Holm column, `e12_event_windows.csv` (114 rows, 24 columns) and `e12_monitor_summary.csv` (alarms, up, down, monitored share).
2. `nice -n 10 python experiments/e22_guaranteed_events.py run --procs=2` (06:05 UTC, 2 min 5 s): the gate again, then the nine new arm and coin jobs (10 to 11 s each for P1 and S1, 40 s each for S2), the S2 check against `e14g_e12_tests.csv`, all outputs and the figure.
3. Post hoc consistency check (not in the pre-registration): S1's alarm times from 21 January 2024 to 30 June 2025 against E19's committed list at band 0.25 (`e19_cs_events.csv`, out-of-sample period).
4. Post hoc description: the dates of the BTC segments whose increase side could not alarm (one scratch rerun of P1 and S1 on BTC; nothing saved to the repository).

**RESULTS.** [REAL]
* Reproduction: every compared value equal (0 mismatches in 840 test cells plus the 2 Holm values of E12's primary family, 2,736 window cells and 12 summary cells; `e22_reproduction.csv`). S2 equals E14 Q14g's awake-B rows exactly (24 cells). S1 equals E19's list over the shared period (285, 290 and 260 alarms).
* **Primary test passes:** P1, BTC, FOMC 8 of 16 against a matched control rate of 10.9%, p = 0.00017, Holm 0.00033 (E12 plug-in 9 of 16 against 13.9%, Holm 0.00025). CPI on BTC under P1: 5 of 22 against 5.9%, Holm 0.0097 (secondary).
* Secondary: P1 FOMC on ETH and SOL Holm 2.0e-5 and 1.7e-5, CPI 0.075 and 0.075; S1 FOMC Holm at most 0.0020 on all coins, CPI 0.12 (BTC), 0.0069 (ETH), 0.090 (SOL); S2 every cell Holm at most 0.00080.
* Watched releases: P1 80 of 114, S1 77, S2 114, P0 55. One BTC FOMC statement (29 January 2025) fell in a blind segment of P1 and S1 (τ² / m_up = 0.948; segment 27 to 30 January 2025, 3.25 days). S1 had a second blind BTC segment (22 to 27 September 2025, 5.19 days) with no release in it.
* Per-day rates: P1 alarmed in 48.1% to 53.5% of its monitored periods (bound 5% under the tolerance), 0.92 to 1.15 alarms per monitored day (long-run bound 0.0404); S1 62.7% to 71.0% of first monitored days; S2 42.2% to 58.7%; the plug-in 88.8% to 91.6%.

**IMPROVEMENTS.** The paper's one pre-specified primary real-data test now has a version run with a monitor that carries a proved per-day bound, pre-registered and reproduced from E12's own code.

**REMAINING ISSUES.**
* The bound is not in force on these data: the tolerance fails on most real days (the per-period alarm share is ten times the bound), so E22 shows that the release contrast holds for a monitor that carries the guarantee, not that the guarantee was exercised.
* The CPI cell on BTC passes under P1 but not under S1 or the plug-in, with the same or fewer releases flagged; its significance comes from a lower matched control rate and depends on the configuration.
* P1 changes the baseline, A and the restart together; only the restart is isolated (S1).
* The confidence-sequence arms alarm later at FOMC statements (median 40.5 minutes on BTC against 15 for the plug-in; descriptive, not pre-registered).
* Nothing failed in the run. No design choice was changed after the results.

**NEXT ITERATION.** None planned. For the paper: replace "what the guarantee buys" by a statement of what E22 shows, and report P1's FOMC result next to E12's.
