## Iteration 1. Replay page built from a checked rerun of E18's primary design

**OBJECTIVE.** Show a statistics reader, minute by minute, what the e-detector and its rate-matched competitors saw on a landmark day: the clipped statistic, both wealth paths against 2A, the recalibration and blind periods, and every method's alarms, with nothing on the page that E18 did not compute.

**PROBLEM IDENTIFIED.** (1) E18 saved alarm lists and outcomes, not the e-detector's statistic or wealth, so the page needs a rerun, and a rerun can silently differ. (2) The competitors' alarms depend on the whole stream through their pauses, so BOCPD has to run over the full two-year Nifty stream and the full half-year BTC stream (10 jobs of 2.5 to 6 minutes each). (3) The raw data may not be committed, and the page must not let a reader rebuild prices or raw returns. (4) `VolatilityMonitor` resets both wealths at an alarm, so the crossing value is lost unless it is read before the reset. (5) pandas 3 stores these time stamps in microseconds, so a nanosecond conversion to epoch minutes was wrong (caught by the test).

**HYPOTHESIS.** Calling E18's own functions (`nifty_stream`, `prepare`, `bocpd_job`, `competitor_stats`, `window_outcomes`, `method_names`, `MARKETS`, `BAND`, `EVENTS`) with the thresholds read back exactly from `results/e18_thresholds.csv` reproduces E18's alarms bit for bit, and a check against `results/e18_alarms.csv` and `results/e18_outcomes.csv` makes any drift stop the build. A subclass that only extends `_reset` keeps the monitor's decisions unchanged while recording the wealth at the alarm.

**CHANGES IMPLEMENTED.** `scripts/build_replay.py`: pure functions `edetector_trace` (streams `VolatilityMonitor` through `TracingMonitor`, records the state when each return arrives and both wealths after it), `clipped_statistic` (Y_t = min(u_t², τ²)/m0 and B = τ²/m0 with the baseline of the last segment started, the previous baseline during a recalibration), `intervals`, `pause_mask` (t + 1 to t + dead after each competitor alarm, as in `scan_alarms`), `check_alarm_lists` (raises on any difference, naming the alarms only in the rerun or only in the CSV), `window_payload`, `encode_times` and `render_html`; the rerun (`rerun_day`) for N1 and C3 with a check that the raw files exist, the BOCPD arrays computed with E18's `bocpd_job` (two processes, optional cache folder outside the repository), thresholds read with `float_precision="round_trip"`, and the checks; the E18 headline sentence and table rows quoted on the page are checked to appear verbatim in `docs/report_parts/E18.md`. The page: one HTML file, no external resource, canvas drawn with plain JavaScript, light and dark themes from CSS variables (system setting and a toggle), play, pause, step, speed, window width and bookmarks, a live caption (clock time, state, wealth against 2A, competitors watching), four stacked panels on one time axis and a compressed strip of the 7 days before T to 24 hours after, a methods table with thresholds and E18's outcomes, and a short explanation. Embedded data: Y_t (3 significant digits), the two wealth paths (4 digits), B per segment, thresholds, alarm positions and directions, recalibration and pause intervals, times as run-length-coded epoch minutes; no price and no return. `tests/test_replay.py`: 6 tests on a synthetic stream of 2,600 returns (sd 1, then 2, then 0.6, with missing values and one jump).

**EXPERIMENTS PERFORMED.** `nice -n 10 python scripts/build_replay.py --cache <scratch folder>` (BOCPD: N1 4 jobs of 158 to 191 seconds, C3 6 jobs of 235 to 415 seconds; 355 and 918 seconds for the two days, two processes on a shared machine; later builds from the cache take about 10 seconds per day). Headless rendering with Playwright at 1280 and 390 pixels, light and dark, with a check for horizontal overflow and for any network request. As a side check, the four Nifty BOCPD arrays of the rerun were compared with the arrays E18 saved in its cache: bitwise identical.

**RESULTS.** [REAL] N1: all 7 methods (e-detector and 6 competitor configurations) have exactly E18's alarms in the window (2, 2, 2, 2, 2, 3 and 1 alarms) and the same pre-registered outcomes; the e-detector was monitoring at T (resumed 3 June 15:19 IST after its up alarm of 30 May 15:18) and raised an up alarm at 09:26 IST, 11 minutes after the open. C3 (BTC): all 10 methods (e-detector and 9 competitor configurations) have exactly E18's alarms in the window (5, 5, 4, 4, 6, 6, 5, 5, 6 and 4 alarms) and the same outcomes; the e-detector was recalibrating at T after its up alarm at 15:30 UTC (back 11 October 15:31), raised no up alarm in the 24 hours after T and next a down alarm at 16:57 UTC on 11 October; 1 of 9 competitor configurations was watching at T (the scan with k = 1440, up alarm at 21:03 UTC, 63 minutes after 20:00 and 6 after 20:57). Page: 290,551 bytes (0.29 MB), both days, the 7 days before T at full one-minute resolution (2,250 Nifty bars and 11,520 BTC minutes), no horizontal overflow at 390 pixels, no console error and no network request in the headless render.

**IMPROVEMENTS.** A reader can watch the blind period happen: on N1 the monitor is watching at the open and alarms within 11 minutes; on C3 it alarms at 15:30 UTC on the earlier tariff threat and is recalibrating through the cascade. Every number on the page comes from a computation checked against E18's CSV files at build time.

**REMAINING ISSUES.** One Nifty day and one coin; the other 16 E18 cases are not on the page (adding a day needs only an entry in `DAYS`, and for the H2 2024 and H1 2025 crypto streams a line in `load_stream`). During a recalibration panel 1 shows Y against the previous baseline, which the monitor does not use; the page says so. The build needs the raw data and about 15 minutes of BOCPD the first time. The E18 quotes are hard-coded and the build stops if `docs/report_parts/E18.md` changes them.
