## 1. Recommendation

**Minor revision, provided that K1 is adopted as given below.**

The three major points of the second report are resolved. The rerun of the FOMC and CPI test with a proved monitor (E22) was pre-registered before any of its real-data runs and is reported next to the plug-in result; the supplement now defines the rank detector and the running truncation level and proves their validity; and the unread references are marked. Every new or changed proof is correct; three have small gaps that one clause each closes (k7, k8, k9), one needs a condition on $B$ (k10), and one can be stated with a weaker hypothesis and an assumption-free companion (k6).

One major point is new. After this version was written, the authors ran a pre-registered test of the FOMC and CPI contrast on the releases of 2026, which no analysis had seen (E23), and it failed. The paper does not mention it, and its abstract still presents the 2024 to 2025 FOMC contrast as its real-data result. K1 gives the replacement text for every place that needs it. It needs no new analysis and no new proof, and the theory, which is the paper's main contribution, is unaffected. If the authors adopt K1 as given, or with the same content, I do not need to see the paper again. If the FOMC contrast is kept as confirmed evidence in any form, my recommendation becomes major revision.

## 6. Checked and correct

**New or changed proofs.**

* *Window bound (line 650): correct.* My derivation: $\lbrace b_i \le s \le b_i + m - 1\rbrace \in \mathcal{F}_{b_i}$; a restart only lowers the statistic, so an alarm in $(s, s + m]$ needs $\tilde M \ge A$ there; Ville's inequality from $s$ and Lemma 2 from $b_i$ give $(\iota + m + s - b_i)/A \le (2m + \iota - 1)/A$ given $\mathcal{F}_{b_i}$. No bound holds given $\mathcal{F}_s$, as the paper says. This agrees with item (b) of the second report.
* *Corollary S14 (lines 652 to 659): correct as stated.* $\Omega_G \in \mathcal{F}_{T_0} \subseteq \mathcal{F}_{b_i}$, $S_t$ is a nonnegative supermartingale with $S_{b_i} \le 1_{\Omega_G}(\iota + m)$, and Ville's inequality gives the display. The marginal reading of line 659 is right: given the observed past, $\mathbb{P}(\Omega_G^c \mid \text{past})$ can exceed $\delta$. It matches Corollary 1 of `docs/theory_per_period.md` (same hypothesis, $T < t \le b'$) and claims no more. The weaker hypothesis and the assumption-free version of k6 follow from the same proof.
* *Corollary 18 used for E22: correct.* With $h = m$ the proof uses the tolerance only during the first $m$ monitored steps, so it holds as stated; `docs/math.md` Corollary 6.6 is stated with $h = \infty$, which is stronger than needed. Scaling at a day boundary only lowers $W$. The long-run bound per 1440 monitored minutes is $(\delta/D + 1440/A)/(1 - \delta)$ with $D$ the monitored minutes over 1440: 0.0404340 for BTC ($D = 337.7125$), 0.0404346 for ETH ($D = 330$) and 0.0404314 for SOL ($D = 369$), as in `results/e22_monitor_summary.csv`. It sets $m$ to the realised number of monitored minutes, which is acceptable for a benchmark that the observed rate exceeds 23 to 28 times.
* *Proposition S15 and Theorem S16 (lines 699 to 707): correct.* In (b) the induction over segments needs the p-values of each new segment to be i.i.d. uniform and independent of $\mathcal{F}_{T_k}$; the hypothesis gives this, and the de Finetti argument for infinite exchangeable sequences is right. The band factor $1/(1 + b)$ gives the supermartingale.
* *Lemma S17 (line 711): correct*, with the conditioning of k9. Line 715 then follows.
* *Theorem S18 (lines 717 to 721): correct* with the gap of k8. Every factor is positive on every path ($f^{\uparrow} \ge 1 - \lambda$, $f^{\downarrow} \ge 1 - s_l$ since $X_t \le \tau_t^2$), $G = D^c \in \mathcal{F}_{T_0}$, and Steps 2 to 4 of the proof of Theorem 16 need only factors with conditional mean at most 1 on an event known at $T_0$. * *Theorem S19 (lines 723 to 727): correct* with the citation of k7. $\lbrace N > e(j)\rbrace \in \mathcal{F}_{e(j)} \subseteq \mathcal{F}_{j-1}$, so each stopped chain is a nonnegative supermartingale from $j$ started at most at 1; $M'_t - t$ is a supermartingale; $M' = M$ on $\lbrace N = \infty\rbrace$; the run-length bound $\mathbb{E}[\vartheta] \ge A(1 - \delta)$ follows from optional stopping, and the count from the structure of the proof of Corollary 18.
