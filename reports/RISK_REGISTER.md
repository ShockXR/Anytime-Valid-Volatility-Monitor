# Risk register (Agent 4): avmonitor

Summary in plain language: the monitor's main promise, a limit on false alarms, held in every independent-returns test I ran with new random seeds. The risks are therefore about where the promise stops applying (clustered volatility, leftover intraday patterns, a dirty calibration window), about the price paid for it (slow detection), and about the package not checking its inputs. Likelihood is given only where I measured it. Severity is for a user relying on the 5% claim.

| Id | Risk | Severity | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| R-B1 | Volatility clustering (GARCH-like) breaks the false-alarm bound for every variant, including the README setting | High | Common in real intraday returns | 7% to 47% false alarms within a day for the README setting (GARCH 0.05/0.90 and 0.08/0.90), 38% to 87% for the known-baseline detector, against a 5% claim | Use the forecast-relative mode on clustered data; state the limit beside the claim (A4-B-006) | OPEN, measured with fresh seeds [VERIFIED] |
| R-B2 | Intraday seasonality not removed | High | Certain if deseasonalisation is skipped | 54% (known baseline) and 57% (plug-in, no band) false alarms within a day; the README band brings it to 2.4% and the confidence sequence to 3.2% | Always use `intraday_scale` from past days; keep the band | PARTLY MITIGATED by the band [VERIFIED] |
| R-B3 | Plug-in baseline is only approximately valid | Medium | Certain over several days | 26.7% to 28.0% within five days against a nominal 25% (within one day at most 3.1%) | Use a band or `baseline="cs"` for any claim over more than a day (A4-B-008) | OPEN, documented as approximate [VERIFIED] |
| R-B4 | The headline setting is slow and misses small changes | Medium | Certain | Variance doubling: median 112 steps (Gaussian LR rule 43); 1.5x increase 506 steps; fall to 2/3 of variance missed in 23% of runs within five days | Band 0.1 (71 and 152 steps, 1.2% false alarms over five days) or cs baseline; say so in the pitch (A4-B-009) | OPEN, a design trade-off [VERIFIED] |
| R-B5 | Contaminated calibration window | Medium | Depends on data cleanliness | 25 outliers of 15 sd in a 500-step window: 68.7% false alarms within 2,500 steps; 10 outliers: 0.7% | Clean or cap extreme values before calibration; check the share of extreme returns in the window (A4-B-007) | OPEN [VERIFIED] |
| R-B6 | Truncation constant too small or absent | Medium | Low (user choice) | c at or below 1: 7% of doublings and 47% of halvings detected; no truncation: 0% of decreases | Validate c between 1.5 and 6 (A4-B-010) | OPEN [VERIFIED] |
| R-B7 | Silent blindness: all-NaN or constant-price input, c = NaN | Medium | Low-Medium (frozen feeds, holidays) | The monitor never alarms and never says so | Warn or expose a status after repeated calibration restarts (A4-B-005) | FIXED 4 Nov 2026 (EXECUTION_SUMMARY.md follow-up) |
| R-B8 | No input validation of parameters | Medium | Low | cal_len 1 to 10 gives 8 to 12 alarms in 3,000 null steps; threshold 0 or 1 alarms at once; null_band 1 or more disables the decrease side | Validate in `__init__` (A4-B-003) | OPEN [VERIFIED] |
| R-B9 | Duplicate timestamps resolved arbitrarily by the Bitstamp loader | Medium | Low on the committed data (the real files had no duplicates in the 3 months checked) | The wrong bar can survive, silently | Stable sort, report count (A4-B-001) | FIXED 4 Nov 2026 (EXECUTION_SUMMARY.md follow-up) |
| R-B10 | Non-positive prices not masked by the Bitstamp and Nifty loaders | Medium | Low | Infinite or NaN returns with only a warning | Mask and report as the Binance loader does (A4-B-002) | FIXED 4 Nov 2026 (EXECUTION_SUMMARY.md follow-up) |
| R-B11 | Missing minutes in a Bitstamp file become multi-minute returns | Low | Low (the source fills gaps) | A 4-minute return treated as 1 minute | Insert NaN rows for gaps as the Binance loader does | OPEN [VERIFIED] |
| R-B12 | Restart loses state | Low | Certain on restart | Pickle and deepcopy resume exactly; a cold restart recalibrates and cannot see a shift that already happened | Add save/load, document that calibration must not be re-run on post-shift data | OPEN [VERIFIED] |
| R-B13 | Non-numeric input fails late | Low | Low | TypeError or ValueError only when calibration ends | Coerce in `update` (A4-B-004) | OPEN [VERIFIED] |
| R-B14 | Data provenance: third-party GitHub mirrors | Low-Medium | Unknown | BTC 2024-01 to 2024-03 files equal the manifest hashes and are clean; other months, coins, Nifty and Bitstamp files not re-downloaded | Run `scripts/download_data.py` and compare hashes before any new claim | UNVERIFIED for 137 of 140 files |
| R-B15 | Results on real data (event studies, pre-registered live session) | Medium | n/a | Not rerun by me; the README itself says the fresh-release test was not confirmed | Keep the exploratory label | UNVERIFIED |
| R-B16 | Heavy tails with infinite variance | Low | Real data are heavy tailed | Pareto(1.5) and Student t3 stayed inside the bound (at most 1.7% within a day for the known baseline); the Gaussian LR rule failed (88% to 100%) | None needed for the guaranteed variants | CLOSED [VERIFIED] |

Root-cause classes: R-B1, R-B2, R-B5 invalid assumption; R-B3 model deficiency (approximation); R-B4 design trade-off; R-B6 to R-B13 implementation gaps; R-B9 to R-B11 and R-B14 data problems. No evaluation error or visualization issue was found in this phase; environment limit: only three months of real data were checked.
